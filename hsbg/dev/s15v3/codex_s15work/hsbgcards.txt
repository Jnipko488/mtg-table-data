37.2 Season 15: Warped Empire — Season 15 — Hearthstone Battlegrounds
Battlegrounds Season 15 brings back the Timewarped Tavern with three new keywords, two new heroes and 77 new cards — 66 minions, 9 Tavern spells, a new Deity and a token — while Trinkets and Dark Gifts rotate out. Live with Patch 37.2 on December 1.
Get ready for Season 15 of Battlegrounds: Warped Empire! This new Battlegrounds season launches December 1 with three new keywords, two new heroes, the return of the Timewarped Tavern, and new cards for all Minion types.
Complete Hearthstone Battlegrounds Season 15 patch notes for update 37.2. This Season 15 update includes 516 card changes across 27 categories: 146 removed, 171 returning, 79 changed, 162 new cards.
Hearthstone Battlegrounds Season 15 brings major changes to the tavern including new minions and balance updates. Browse all Season 15 card changes below.
Leaving Mechanics (3)
Trinkets rotate out of Battlegrounds as the Timewarped Tavern returns.
Dark Gifts rotate out of Battlegrounds.
Stealth leaves the pool of Bonus Keywords for the time being, making room for Resilient.
Hero Updates (4)
BananaramaText: At the start of your turn, get 2 Bananas and give everyone else one. → At the start of each turn, get 2 Tavern Dish Bananas and give everyone else one.
Zerek, Master Cloner: Timewarp restriction removed.
Sindragosa and Millhouse Manastorm: Now banned from Pirate lobbies.
Murozond, Unbounded and Morchie: No longer banned from Aberration lobbies.

Dev Insights (16)
Well met!
With Battlegrounds Season 15, Warped Empire, there are a lot of changes coming to the Tavern. We’re bringing back the Timewarped Tavern system (with 70+ new cards!), adding three new Keywords, and welcoming two new Heroes to the existing roster.
Today, we wanted to take some time to talk about the design thinking behind these additions, starting with a returning favorite, the Timewarped Tavern.
This is the first time we’re returning the Timewarped Tavern since it was originally introduced way back in Season 12. The feedback last time was overall very positive, so we didn’t make any significant changes to how the system functions. You’ll still be offered a selection of Timewarped cards on Turns 6 and 9 to buy using Chronum, and those offerings will be based on what you’ve been playing that game. Rather than spending our time redesigning the core functionality of the system, we instead focused on expanding and further curating the pool of Timewarped cards.
We first took a pass at all existing Timewarped cards to decide which felt right to return. The majority of cards we chose not to return were cut for one of the two following reasons. The first reason is pick rate. In general, we want players to be offered cards they are excited to play with. However, some cards were being passed over a bit too often, which usually indicates that players don't find them fun or powerful enough. For some of those cards, such as Timewarped Sensei, Timewarped Lubber, or Timewarped Goldrinn, we found ways to buff them and keep them in the pool. But for others, such as Timewarped Ring, Timewarped Dragonling, and Timewarped Deadstomper, we chose to cut them instead to make space for new cards. — Timewarped Sensei, Timewarped Lubber, Timewarped Goldrinn, Timewarped Ring, Timewarped Dragonling, Timewarped Deadstomper
The other reason is unintended interactions. Some cards, such as Timewarped Embalmer, Timewarped Conch, and Timewarped Whirl-O-Tron, could create unfun or unfair board states when combined in specific ways. So, we chose to remove those cards to make the game more enjoyable for everyone. — Timewarped Embalmer, Timewarped Conch, Timewarped Whirl-O-Tron
After reviewing the existing cards, we found about 120 that we felt were worth bringing back. We then set a goal for how many new cards we wanted to create. As a rough target, we wanted to have around 5 new/returning Minor Timewarped cards and around 7 new/returning Major Timewarped cards in the pool for each minion type. This should give players enough variety that they don't see the same cards from game to game without making the pool so large that it becomes unreasonable to wrap your head around. We also wanted to have as many neutral Timewarped cards as possible, as those cards appear more frequently than typed cards, so you need a lot of them to avoid seeing the same ones all the time.
Quite a few of the new Timewarped cards, such as Timewarped Fruit Vendor, Timewarped Morchie, and Timewarped Tickatus, take advantage of the Activate Keyword we introduced last season. Some older cards, such as Timewarped Devourer, Timewarped Tipper, and Timewarped Bloodbinder, were updated to use Activate to make them smoother to play with. We also managed to sneak in a few Timewarped cards that use Dark Gifts, such as Timewarped Telescope and Timewarped Wallow. We also added a new very rare 3-Chronum card, Timewarped Murozond, if you can find a way to get them. — Timewarped Fruit Vendor, Timewarped Morchie, Timewarped Tickatus, Timewarped Devourer, Timewarped Tipper, Timewarped Bloodbinder, Timewarped Telescope, Timewarped Wallow, Timewarped Murozond
Season 15 introduces three new Keywords: Resilient, Mission, and Elemental Invocation.
The first new Keyword is Resilient. Resilient minions take half damage when attacked. This damage is rounded up, so it would take 5 damage when hit by a 9-Attack minion. Keep in mind that damage while attacking is not reduced, so you'll want to keep Resilient minions on the right side of your board so they don't attack too early.
We had a few goals when designing Resilient:
1. We wanted it to be simple and easy to understand;
2. We wanted it to feel useful on any minion;
3. We wanted it to add texture during combat;
4. We wanted it to be an evergreen keyword we could use in future sets; and
5. We wanted it to be something we could add to the Bonus Keyword pool.
On that last point, we've felt for a while that Stealth has been a bit awkward as part of the Bonus Keyword pool. Often, Stealth doesn't feel like a particularly powerful keyword and can sometime be undesired (such as on Taunt minions). However, when applied to very specific minions (like Titus Rivendare) or applied across your whole board, it can feel incredibly frustrating to face. For this reason, we decided to swap it out and replace it with Resilient to help make Bonus Keywords feel consistently useful.
In general, we expect Resilient to be a fairly rare Keyword, closer to the prevalence of Windfury or Venomous rather than Taunt or Reborn. Most of the Resilient minions in this patch are Demons, but we expect to spread the keyword around in future updates based on community feedback. — Hardy Felhound, Imp-erial, Nonchalant Napper
Our next new Keyword is Mission. Missions are basically like minion Quests – complete a specific goal for a one-time reward. You have to have the minion in play in order to complete its Mission, and each Mission can only be completed once. However, if you manage to triple a Mission minion, the Mission will reset and can be completed again! Don't worry though; if you only made partial progress on a Mission minion that triples, it'll keep that progress.
We tried making lots of different Mission cards during development and found that they were the most fun during the early-mid game when your board isn't fully locked in yet. We also wanted to avoid having too many Missions that could be completed as soon as you buy them, as then they just feel like awkward Battlecry minions.
Since Mission cards can only be completed once, it gave us space to make more powerful effects than what you'd typically see at that point of the game if they were repeatable (such as Obsessive Artisan or Greymist Infiltrator). Some older minions, such as Free Travel Winner, Treasure Parrot, and Scarlet Survivor, were updated to use the new Mission Keyword as their old effects were essentially already Missions. — Scarlet Survivor, Patron Gatherer, Obsessive Artisan, Free Travel Winner, Ambitious Cannoneer, Treasure Parrot, Tenacious Thorn-witch, Crypt Crawler, Greymist Infiltrator, Crafty Cookfire, Loti, Devilsaur Form
Our last Keyword, Elemental Invocation, is similar to some of our other type-specific Keywords like Bounty or Chromadrake. It grants you one of four different Elemental Invocations that give stats and a temporary Bonus Keyword to an Elemental. We had a bit of a theme around Bonus Keywords this set, in particular in Murlocs, Quilboar, and Elementals, as well as the new Resilient Keyword. We originally tried having Resilient as one of the possible Bonus Keywords off of Elemental Invocations, but it was a bit much to have that much Resilience. — Effusive Dancer, Tsunami Surfer, Invocation of Earth, Invocation of Fire, Invocation of Air, Invocation of Water
We're also making a change to Deity that gives us more flexibility when balancing individual Deities in the future. The updated keyword is:
Deity (x) - After X friendly Aberrations die each combat, awaken a X/Y [Deity name]
Previously, all Deities were summoned after 4 Aberrations died. This feels reasonable for both of our existing Deities, C'Thun and Y'Shaarj. But for our new Deity, N'Zoth, 4 deaths felt too slow given how many times N'Zoth can be resummoned. By making the number of deaths variable between Deities, we have more flexibility to balance each of them. It also gives potential design space to make Deities in the future that summon after only 1 or 2 deaths or summon after 5 or more deaths. — C'Thun, Y'Shaarj, N'Zoth
Season 15 also introduces two new Heroes, Eternus and Vanessa, the Ringleader, each with their own approach to the game.
Typically whenever we make a new or returning system, we like to make heroes that can use that system in unique ways. We have lots of Heroes like this, such as Marin, Morchie, and Sire, and they are typically some of our most popular Heroes. For Eternus, we wanted to make a new Timewarped Hero that played a little different than Morchie/Murozond. We took inspiration from Thorim and decided to give you your Timewarped cards right at the start of the game so that you can plan your strategy early. We tried a few different unlock conditions for the Timewarped cards, but we liked the gameplay of spending gold because there's a predictable range for when it can be completed while still providing opportunity for skill expression. — Eternus, Dawn of the Infinite
Vanessa’s design took a different approach, as her Hero Power doesn't relate to any of our systems. Instead, we wanted her to feel like a flexible combo Hero. We wanted players to be able to make small turns where they can get an early Tier 3 or 4 minion, while also providing opportunity for a big popoff turn where you get an early Tier 6. In the late game, when you have more gold at your disposal, players can then use it to dig for specific minions on specific Tiers depending on what they need. — Vanessa the Ringleader, Strategic Edge

37.2 Season 15: Warped Empire
Season 15
Published November 23, 2026
Battlegrounds Season 15 brings back the Timewarped Tavern with three new keywords, two new heroes and 77 new cards — 66 minions, 9 Tavern spells, a new Deity and a token — while Trinkets and Dark Gifts rotate out. Live with Patch 37.2 on December 1.
Dev Insights
Get ready for Season 15 of Battlegrounds: Warped Empire! This new Battlegrounds season launches December 1 with three new keywords, two new heroes, the return of the Timewarped Tavern, and new cards for all Minion types.
Well met!
With Battlegrounds Season 15, Warped Empire, there are a lot of changes coming to the Tavern. We’re bringing back the Timewarped Tavern system (with 70+ new cards!), adding three new Keywords, and welcoming two new Heroes to the existing roster.
Today, we wanted to take some time to talk about the design thinking behind these additions, starting with a returning favorite, the Timewarped Tavern.
The Timewarped Tavern Returns
This is the first time we’re returning the Timewarped Tavern since it was originally introduced way back in Season 12. The feedback last time was overall very positive, so we didn’t make any significant changes to how the system functions. You’ll still be offered a selection of Timewarped cards on Turns 6 and 9 to buy using Chronum, and those offerings will be based on what you’ve been playing that game. Rather than spending our time redesigning the core functionality of the system, we instead focused on expanding and further curating the pool of Timewarped cards.
We first took a pass at all existing Timewarped cards to decide which felt right to return. The majority of cards we chose not to return were cut for one of the two following reasons. The first reason is pick rate. In general, we want players to be offered cards they are excited to play with. However, some cards were being passed over a bit too often, which usually indicates that players don't find them fun or powerful enough. For some of those cards, such as Timewarped Sensei, Timewarped Lubber, or Timewarped Goldrinn, we found ways to buff them and keep them in the pool. But for others, such as Timewarped Ring, Timewarped Dragonling, and Timewarped Deadstomper, we chose to cut them instead to make space for new cards.
The other reason is unintended interactions. Some cards, such as Timewarped Embalmer, Timewarped Conch, and Timewarped Whirl-O-Tron, could create unfun or unfair board states when combined in specific ways. So, we chose to remove those cards to make the game more enjoyable for everyone.
After reviewing the existing cards, we found about 120 that we felt were worth bringing back. We then set a goal for how many new cards we wanted to create. As a rough target, we wanted to have around 5 new/returning Minor Timewarped cards and around 7 new/returning Major Timewarped cards in the pool for each minion type. This should give players enough variety that they don't see the same cards from game to game without making the pool so large that it becomes unreasonable to wrap your head around. We also wanted to have as many neutral Timewarped cards as possible, as those cards appear more frequently than typed cards, so you need a lot of them to avoid seeing the same ones all the time.
Quite a few of the new Timewarped cards, such as Timewarped Fruit Vendor, Timewarped Morchie, and Timewarped Tickatus, take advantage of the Activate Keyword we introduced last season. Some older cards, such as Timewarped Devourer, Timewarped Tipper, and Timewarped Bloodbinder, were updated to use Activate to make them smoother to play with. We also managed to sneak in a few Timewarped cards that use Dark Gifts, such as Timewarped Telescope and Timewarped Wallow. We also added a new very rare 3-Chronum card, Timewarped Murozond, if you can find a way to get them.
New Keywords
Season 15 introduces three new Keywords: Resilient, Mission, and Elemental Invocation.
Resilient
The first new Keyword is Resilient. Resilient minions take half damage when attacked. This damage is rounded up, so it would take 5 damage when hit by a 9-Attack minion. Keep in mind that damage while attacking is not reduced, so you'll want to keep Resilient minions on the right side of your board so they don't attack too early.
We had a few goals when designing Resilient:
1. We wanted it to be simple and easy to understand;
2. We wanted it to feel useful on any minion;
3. We wanted it to add texture during combat;
4. We wanted it to be an evergreen keyword we could use in future sets; and
5. We wanted it to be something we could add to the Bonus Keyword pool.
On that last point, we've felt for a while that Stealth has been a bit awkward as part of the Bonus Keyword pool. Often, Stealth doesn't feel like a particularly powerful keyword and can sometime be undesired (such as on Taunt minions). However, when applied to very specific minions (like Titus Rivendare) or applied across your whole board, it can feel incredibly frustrating to face. For this reason, we decided to swap it out and replace it with Resilient to help make Bonus Keywords feel consistently useful.
In general, we expect Resilient to be a fairly rare Keyword, closer to the prevalence of Windfury or Venomous rather than Taunt or Reborn. Most of the Resilient minions in this patch are Demons, but we expect to spread the keyword around in future updates based on community feedback.
Mission
Our next new Keyword is Mission. Missions are basically like minion Quests – complete a specific goal for a one-time reward. You have to have the minion in play in order to complete its Mission, and each Mission can only be completed once. However, if you manage to triple a Mission minion, the Mission will reset and can be completed again! Don't worry though; if you only made partial progress on a Mission minion that triples, it'll keep that progress.
We tried making lots of different Mission cards during development and found that they were the most fun during the early-mid game when your board isn't fully locked in yet. We also wanted to avoid having too many Missions that could be completed as soon as you buy them, as then they just feel like awkward Battlecry minions.
Since Mission cards can only be completed once, it gave us space to make more powerful effects than what you'd typically see at that point of the game if they were repeatable (such as Obsessive Artisan or Greymist Infiltrator). Some older minions, such as Free Travel Winner, Treasure Parrot, and Scarlet Survivor, were updated to use the new Mission Keyword as their old effects were essentially already Missions.
Elemental Invocation
Our last Keyword, Elemental Invocation, is similar to some of our other type-specific Keywords like Bounty or Chromadrake. It grants you one of four different Elemental Invocations that give stats and a temporary Bonus Keyword to an Elemental. We had a bit of a theme around Bonus Keywords this set, in particular in Murlocs, Quilboar, and Elementals, as well as the new Resilient Keyword. We originally tried having Resilient as one of the possible Bonus Keywords off of Elemental Invocations, but it was a bit much to have that much Resilience.
Updated Keyword – Deity
We're also making a change to Deity that gives us more flexibility when balancing individual Deities in the future. The updated keyword is:
Deity (x) - After X friendly Aberrations die each combat, awaken a X/Y [Deity name]
Previously, all Deities were summoned after 4 Aberrations died. This feels reasonable for both of our existing Deities, C'Thun and Y'Shaarj. But for our new Deity, N'Zoth, 4 deaths felt too slow given how many times N'Zoth can be resummoned. By making the number of deaths variable between Deities, we have more flexibility to balance each of them. It also gives potential design space to make Deities in the future that summon after only 1 or 2 deaths or summon after 5 or more deaths.
New Heroes: Designing Eternus and Vanessa
Season 15 also introduces two new Heroes, Eternus and Vanessa, the Ringleader, each with their own approach to the game.
Eternus
Typically whenever we make a new or returning system, we like to make heroes that can use that system in unique ways. We have lots of Heroes like this, such as Marin, Morchie, and Sire, and they are typically some of our most popular Heroes. For Eternus, we wanted to make a new Timewarped Hero that played a little different than Morchie/Murozond. We took inspiration from Thorim and decided to give you your Timewarped cards right at the start of the game so that you can plan your strategy early. We tried a few different unlock conditions for the Timewarped cards, but we liked the gameplay of spending gold because there's a predictable range for when it can be completed while still providing opportunity for skill expression.
Vanessa the Ringleader
Vanessa’s design took a different approach, as her Hero Power doesn't relate to any of our systems. Instead, we wanted her to feel like a flexible combo Hero. We wanted players to be able to make small turns where they can get an early Tier 3 or 4 minion, while also providing opportunity for a big popoff turn where you get an early Tier 6. In the late game, when you have more gold at your disposal, players can then use it to dig for specific minions on specific Tiers depending on what they need.
The above is just some of the thinking behind the changes coming with Battlegrounds Season 15, and we’re looking forward to seeing what you will do with the returning Timewarped Tavern, the new Keywords, and Eternus and Vanessa once 37.2 arrives.
See you in the Tavern!
